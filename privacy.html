Beelink Backups

Privacy policy

Purpose and operator

Beelink Backups is a personal backup utility operated by GitHub user TJReed222 for the owner's own Google account.

Information accessed and used

The utility uses Google's drive.file permission to create and manage its own backup files and folders. It processes file contents and metadata, such as names, sizes, timestamps, identifiers, and checksums, to upload, verify, restore, and manage retention of backups.

Storage and access

Backup data is stored on the owner's devices and in the owner's Google Drive account. OAuth credentials and tokens are stored on the owner's device for authorized transfers. Operational logs may contain filenames and transfer results. Credentials and backup contents are not published on this website.

Sharing and other uses

Google processes backup data to provide Google Drive storage and API services. The operator does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models. Backups are not intentionally shared with other people unless the owner explicitly chooses to share them.

Retention and deletion

Backups remain until the owner deletes them or a configured retention rule removes them. Deleting an original local file does not necessarily delete its backup. Google Drive trash and provider retention rules may also apply.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke the application's access through Google Account third-party connections and remove local OAuth credentials. Revoking access stops future authorized transfers but does not itself delete existing backups.

This website

This informational website has no login form, advertising, or operator-added analytics. It is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect technical information, including visitor IP addresses, under its privacy statement.

Contact and changes

Questions may be submitted through the project issue tracker. Issues are public: do not include credentials or private backup information. This policy will be updated if the utility's data practices change.
